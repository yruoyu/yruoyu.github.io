Ruoyu Yin

Hi, I am a theorist working on

  • monitored quantum dynamics and quantum measurement
  • open quantum systems
  • non-equilibrium statistical physics

My work asks how measurement and dissipation reshape quantum dynamics, and how they can be harnessed to control it.

Currently, I am a postdoctoral researcher in the group of Andreas Dechant at Kyoto University, and PI of a JSPS KAKENHI Start-up grant.
