Hi, I am a theorist interested in

  • monitored quantum dynamics
  • quantum measurements
  • open quantum systems
  • non-equilibrium statistical physics

Much of my work asks how measurements (strong, weak, etc.(?)) and dissipation reshape, or can be used to control quantum dynamics.

Currently, I am a postdoctoral researcher in the group of Andreas Dechant at Kyoto University.
